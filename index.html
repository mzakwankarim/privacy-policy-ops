Privacy Policy for GEP Ops

Last Updated: November 6, 2026
This Privacy Policy describes how Secure LabX / GuardExpertPro ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the GEP Ops mobile application ("App") available on Google Play. GEP Ops is an enterprise security and workforce management application designed to facilitate operational patrol monitoring, shift attendance, and personnel administration for authorized organizations.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal and Organizational Information

When you or your organization creates or manages accounts through GEP Ops, we may collect:

  • User Credentials & Profile Details: Full name, username, official email address, assigned company, role, and encrypted authentication tokens.
  • Personnel & Marshal Registration Data: Name, phone number, identification numbers, and assigned premises/zones.
  • Profile & Identification Photos: Photos taken via your device camera or selected from your photo library specifically for marshal and officer identity verification.

B. Operational & Shift Data

  • Attendance & Patrol Logs: Shift check-in/check-out timestamps, zone/premise assignments, patrol status, and alert/incident logs.

C. Device and Technical Information

To ensure app reliability and notification delivery, we automatically collect:

  • Device Identifiers: Device manufacturer, hardware model, operating system version, and unique installation IDs.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens used solely to dispatch real-time operational alerts and shift notifications.
  • Diagnostics and Crash Logs: Firebase Crashlytics diagnostic data, stack traces, and application state at the time of an unexpected crash.
  • In-App Usage Analytics: Aggregated screen views and interaction events to optimize user interface performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly for operational and business purposes:

  • Service Provision: Authenticating users, managing security zones, tracking attendance, and facilitating premise assignments.
  • Critical Alerts & Push Notifications: Sending notifications for missed patrols, understaffed zones, or urgent incidents.
  • Security & Verification: Confirming officer and marshal identity on duty.
  • System Maintenance: Detecting and repairing software crashes, improving app stability, and preventing unauthorized access.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties for advertising or marketing.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We utilize trusted third-party services that process information on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Google Firebase (Firebase Cloud Messaging & Firebase Crashlytics): Used for push notification routing, crash diagnostic logs, and app stability analysis. Policy reference: Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts: Used to render typography within the mobile application.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Transmission Security: All communication between the App and our backend servers is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Local Storage: Sensitive session tokens and local offline patrol databases are stored within protected application sandbox directories.
  • Access Control: Access to operational data is restricted solely to authorized organizational personnel with designated roles and permissions.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

  • Data Retention: We retain operational data only as long as required by your organization’s contract, service agreement, or applicable legal obligations.
  • Account Deletion & Data Removal: Users wishing to have their account, personal identifiers, or uploaded photos removed may contact their organization's system administrator or email us directly at GuardExpertPro@securelabx.com. Requests will be processed within 30 days, subject to regulatory workforce record-keeping requirements.

6. Children's Privacy

GEP Ops is exclusively intended for authorized adult workforce personnel. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations. The updated policy will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
